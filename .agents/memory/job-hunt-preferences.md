Shortlisting preference order (user-mandated, Aug 2026):
1. English-speaking roles first.
2. Companies offering visa sponsorship / welcoming international candidates.
3. Country priority: Malaysia top, then English-speaking countries (Australia, NZ, Ireland), then Sweden/Switzerland/Poland/Portugal.

CV creation rules (all tailored CVs):
1. Follow the CV norms of the target country.
2. Simple formatting; clean PDF output.
3. ATS-friendly: standard headings, no tables/columns/graphics, keywords mirrored truthfully from the job description.
4. Simple English.
5. Optimize the CV so it gets shortlisted for the specific role.

**How to apply:** Bake these into the ranking and tailoring prompts of the daily job-search pipeline; any new job-hunt or CV feature must honor them.
